Why no synced-passkey branch?

Device-bound passkeys never leave the hardware they were created on (a FIDO2 key, Windows Hello for Business, Authenticator on a phone). Synced passkeys are encrypted and copied across a user's devices via a provider like iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager - convenient, but they don't support attestation, which is why our tenant keeps the passkey profile device-bound only. See Microsoft's comparison in Passkeys (FIDO2) authentication method in Microsoft Entra ID.
